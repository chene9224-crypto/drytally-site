- Legal content: `content/terms.json` and `content/privacy.json`, version 2026-10-24.6

Run `python3 scripts/build.py` to generate the pages. The app bundles byte-identical legal JSON for offline reading. Update both copies and the consent version when material legal content changes; verify the live URLs and the app entry points before distributing an updated app.

No advertising scripts, third-party analytics, remote fonts, diary collection forms or secrets are included. Product images use synthetic sample records. The app source repository is separate and private. Optional Apple CloudKit history sync is off by default and requires valid access and a restart; photo files remain local. The website does not imply that Drytally is already downloadable.

These documents describe the product and its intended US/UK/EEA audience; publication is not a certification of legal compliance, app release readiness, a verified representative appointment, or live purchase processing.

Purchase-service update: the policy now covers configured RevenueCat and Cloudflare purchase-proof verification and provider events; the operator-hosted purchase service receives no diary data. This is not evidence of a completed Apple Sandbox purchase.

Brand direction: drinking records and reduction, with stopping completely as an optional goal. The approved icon uses the full-green C composition (character, beer glass and calendar tally); assets are shared with the app.
